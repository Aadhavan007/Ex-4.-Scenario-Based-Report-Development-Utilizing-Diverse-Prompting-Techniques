Ex-4: Scenario-Based Report Development Utilizing Diverse Prompting Techniques
Objective:

To design and develop an AI-powered chatbot capable of handling customer inquiries, providing support, and improving the overall customer experience in a retail environment. The experiment uses various AI prompting techniques to guide chatbot responses, collect data, and analyze performance.

# Aim:

To implement and test multiple prompting techniques for creating an intelligent AI chatbot that can respond accurately, empathetically, and efficiently to retail customer queries.

# Algorithm / Procedure:

Define the chatbot scenario: A retail company wants an AI assistant to handle product inquiries, complaints, and returns.

Identify the types of prompts to be used: intuitive, structured, role-based, refinement, and multi-turn prompts.

Generate prompts for each scenario to elicit responses from the AI model.

Collect outputs from the AI for each prompt type.

Analyze outputs based on accuracy, empathy, clarity, and relevance.

Refine prompts iteratively to improve chatbot response quality.

Document the final chatbot performance and generate a report summarizing the findings.

Prompt (Using Various Prompting Techniques):

Intuitive Prompt:
“Imagine you are a friendly customer support agent. How would you respond to a customer asking about a delayed order?”

Structured Prompt:
“List five common customer questions in a retail store and provide suitable AI-generated responses for each.”

Role-Based Prompt:
“Act as a customer support AI for an online retail platform. A customer asks: ‘I received a damaged product, what should I do?’”

Refinement Prompt:
“Rewrite the chatbot’s response to make it more polite, concise, and empathetic for a frustrated customer.”

Multi-Turn Dialogue Prompt:
“Customer: ‘Where is my order?’ → Bot: [Generate a professional and helpful response].
Customer: ‘It still hasn’t arrived.’ → Bot: [Provide next response maintaining context].”

# Output (Sample AI Responses):

Intuitive Prompt:
“Hello! I understand your order is delayed. I’m really sorry for the inconvenience. Let me check your order status and provide you with an update.”

Structured Prompt:

Question: “How do I track my order?”
Bot Response: “You can track your order by logging into your account and checking the 'Orders' section.”

Question: “Can I return a product?”
Bot Response: “Yes, you can return products within 30 days of purchase following our return policy.”

Question: “My product arrived damaged.”
Bot Response: “I apologize for the inconvenience. Please provide your order number and we’ll arrange a replacement.”

Question: “Do you have discounts?”
Bot Response: “We have seasonal offers. Check our promotions page for current discounts.”

Question: “How can I contact support?”
Bot Response: “You can reach our support team via chat, email, or phone.”

Role-Based Prompt:
“Hello! I’m sorry your product arrived damaged. Please share your order number, and we’ll arrange a replacement immediately.”

Refinement Prompt:
“Apologies for the inconvenience! I understand this must be frustrating. Kindly provide your order number, and I’ll ensure a replacement is arranged promptly.”

Multi-Turn Dialogue Prompt:

Bot Response 1: “Your order is currently in transit and should arrive in 2–3 business days. Can I help you track it in detail?”

Bot Response 2: “I apologize for the delay. I’ve escalated your order with priority shipping and you will receive a confirmation shortly.”

# Result:

The chatbot responded accurately, empathetically, and consistently across multiple scenarios.

Structured prompts produced the most organized responses, while refinement and role-based prompts improved tone and user satisfaction.

Multi-turn prompts successfully maintained context over multiple exchanges, simulating realistic customer conversations.

The experiment demonstrates that diverse prompting techniques significantly enhance the performance, reliability, and user-friendliness of AI-powered chatbots in retail support environments.
